02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Yields rose across the curve into the weekend, with the 20Y hit hardest. On 08-21 KTB cash yields rose throughout — 2Y +3.5bp (3.707%), 3Y +3.3bp (3.855%), 10Y +4.8bp (4.370%), 20Y +6.3bp (4.665%), 30Y +3.8bp (4.710%) — while 3Y futures −0.09 (103.13) and 10Y futures −0.46 (105.11) fell for a second day. Foreigners split: +2,400 in 3Y futures, −2,512 in the 10Y, with securities firms on the other side of the 3Y at −2,268. Per the latest close report, the short end is weighed down by caution into the August MPC plus seasonal VAT-payment funding pressure, while the ultra-long end draws support from expectations of larger US Treasury buybacks. Today brings the KTB 5Y auction, with the MPC three days out.

The US also sold off, but flattened. On 08-21 2Y +4.9bp (4.238%) and 10Y +2.8bp (4.734%) — the 2Y underperformed, narrowing 2-10 to 49.6bp (−2.1bp). The front end repriced hawkishly ahead of Jackson Hole (8/27~29) and PCE (8/26), with 2Y, 5Y and 7Y auctions lined up this week.

Flatteners: 20-30 has reached its first target, 10-30 stays on. On 08-21 20-30 tightened to 4.5bp (−2.5bp), through the RV first target of 5.4bp, with its z-score down to +0.8σ — richness largely spent, so take profit / trim. 10-30 at 34.0bp (−1.0bp · +1.3σ) is still 4bp from its ~30bp target and stays on. By contrast 3-10 widened to 51.5bp (+1.5bp · +1.5σ), once again the richest — pressure that persists while the front end stays heavy into the MPC. (RV ranks and scores are from the 08-20 model run: 10-30 and 20-30 flatteners at 86 each, 3-10-30 butterfly 79.7; the tactical 10Y short at 45.5 and the 3-10 steepener at 32.9 are avoids.)

Weekly foreign cash flows: long end bought, front end sold. Over 08/17~21 they bought the 10Y on-the-run 26-6 (+9,316), FX stabilization 26-8 (+7,050) and the ultra-long 25-9 (+1,396), against sales of the near-maturity FX stabilization 25-10 (−6,400) and MSBs (−4,500) — cash flows pointing the same way as the ultra-long flattening.
