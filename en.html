02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Bull steepening — heavy foreign buying in 3Y futures. On 08-19 3Y futures +0.17 (103.30) and 10Y futures +0.37 (105.44) rallied. Foreigners bought 14,083 contracts of 3Y futures, while a firmer won (USD/KRW 1,397.7, −14.1) and a sharp drop in semiconductor shares added support. US yields also fell once the 20Y auction cleared — 10Y −5.9bp, 30Y −9.1bp. No major events today; PCE, BOK MPC and Jackson Hole next week.

Curve: Korea re-steepens while the US flattens. 3-10 at +2.2σ (54.8bp) is the richest dislocation, with 3-30 at 94.3bp (1M +21.2bp) and 20-30 at 10.0bp (+1.9σ). The front end (2Y −3.3bp) outperformed and widened the curve again, whereas the US flattened across the board (2-30 −8.3bp) — directions have diverged.

RV ranks 1 and 2: the 10-30 and 20-30 flatteners (86 points each). Targets are 10-30 39.5→28.8bp (full mean 16.1bp) and 20-30 10.0→5.3bp. Insurers bought KRW 1,489bn of 30Y cash, underpinning the ultra-long sector. Rank 3 is the 3-10-30 butterfly (81.1) — long 3Y and 30Y versus short 10Y. By contrast the tactical 10Y short (44.1) and the 3-10 steepener (26.9) are flagged "avoid — no new entries".
