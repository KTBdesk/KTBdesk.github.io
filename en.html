03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Yesterday's correction on the 20Y was priced in a single day. The 20Y benchmark 26-10 cheapened 4.6bp to 4.530 on its own, taking 10-20 from 3.9 to 8.3bp, 20-30 from 12.1 to 9.9bp and the 10-20-30 fly from 8.2 to 1.6bp (−6.6). Its inversion to the old 25-9 narrowed from 8.5 to 6.6bp. We expected an October auction to be the trigger; flows got there first — offshore sold KRW 80.0bn of 26-10 while banks, insurers and pensions together bought only about 30bn.

Still expensive: the 10Y. Still cheap: the 30Y. Oil drives the long end, the Fed holds the front
· 10Y rich — it was the only tenor to cheapen slightly (+0.2bp). 5-10 19.2bp (−3.09σ) · 3-10 41.7bp (−2.33σ)
· The front recovered — 2Y −2.5, 3Y −2.2, 5Y −1.5bp; with the auction behind it the 5Y came back and 5-10 went 17.5 → 19.2bp (+1.7)
· 30Y cheap — +2.4bp (4.629), offshore sold KRW 135.0bn of 26-2, and 10-30 is +6.3bp over three days at 18.2
· The 20Y is most of the way back — the fly at +1.38σ from +2.02σ yesterday

Oil fell again. Saudi Arabia restarted the East-West pipeline and is preparing to resume exports through Yanbu on the Red Sea, easing supply-disruption fears, and Iran floated reopening the Strait of Hormuz if US military pressure eases. WTI is below $90 — a full reversal from last week's triple digits, and a supportive backdrop for bonds.

But the US front end will not fall on oil alone. The UST 2Y was down more than 5bp intraday before retracing to about 4.75%, closing +0.5bp at 4.7545; the 10Y closed +1.1bp (4.9635) and the 30Y +1.4bp (5.2995). While October hike odds hold at 55% and at least one more by year-end at 91%, the 2–5Y cannot take the full benefit of cheaper oil. KR10−US10 at −51.6bp.

Flows — offshore buys futures and sells cash. 3Y +2,882 and 10Y +2,644 lots, both contracts for a second day. Cash went the other way: −KRW 789.1bn of KTBs, concentrated in 2027 maturities (22-1 −100.0 · 25-1 −183.0 · 17-3 −100.0bn) and the ultra-long (−309.0bn: 26-2 −135.0 · 26-10 −80.0 · 22-9 −22.0bn). Insurers and pensions bought +120.0bn of ultra-longs (26-8 +75.5 · 26-2 +28.9 · 26-10 +12.0bn) plus +40.0bn of the 26-8 principal strip.

The futures discount has almost gone. Versus fair value the 3Y is 5.1 ticks cheap and the 10Y just 1.0 tick (5.3 and 6.7 yesterday) — the 10Y closed only +1 tick yet took six ticks out of its discount. Implied − cash: 3Y +15.0bp (60-day +1.75σ) · 10Y +4.2bp (+1.63σ).

One hawkish comment from the BOK. Board member Chang Yong-sung said in his message that vigilance over the build-up of financial imbalances is high. Policy pricing eased slightly: 3Y − base 103.0bp (−2.2) · 1Y swap − base 72.8bp (−1.3). The next BOK meeting is 10/22, then 11/26.

Today is the last session before the holiday. Beyond the MSB tap there is no domestic event; the US 5Y and 7Y auctions (02:00 KST Thu/Fri) and oil pass while Korea is shut, and everything lands on Monday 09/28. Keep positions light and expressed on the curve.
