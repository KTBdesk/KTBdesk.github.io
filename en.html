03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

On 10/12 the 10Y led and the whole curve followed. Cash KTBs went 2Y −5.5 (3.855) · 3Y −7.9 (3.918) · 5Y −7.2 (4.102) · 10Y −11.5 (4.297) · 20Y −6.0 (4.356) · 30Y −7.2 (4.385) · 50Y −7.3bp (4.350). Futures closed +27 ticks at 102.77 (high 102.80) and +101 ticks at 105.41 (high 105.49), never trading below their opens of 102.56 and 104.75. The US was shut for Columbus Day and KOSPI fell 3.65%, KOSDAQ 4.83%, so this was a rally built entirely on domestic flow and risk aversion, with no overnight cue. 3-10 compressed from 41.5 to 37.9bp (60-day z −2.29) while 10-30 retraced from 4.5 to 8.8bp. 10-20 went from 0.4 to 5.9bp in a single session, 20-30 is 2.9, and 5-10 fell from 23.8 to 19.5 (−4.3) — the 10Y out-rallied the 5Y by a wide margin. The 3Y is back to 91.8bp over base, away from 100 again.

Who bought — offshore, in futures; everyone sold cash. Offshore bought 34,789 3Y and 12,126 10Y contracts, roughly KRW 3.5tn and 1.2tn notional, more than the day's KRW 2.4tn 3Y auction. Securities houses were on the other side (−23,199 and −7,950). 3Y open interest rose 8,267 to 480,946 — OI rising on an up day is new length. 10Y open interest fell 3,468 to 262,159 — OI falling on a one-won rally is still short covering. Cash was the mirror image. Offshore sold KRW 645bn of KTBs (1–2Y −228bn, 3–5Y −272bn; MSBs +220bn), pensions −451bn, asset managers −325bn, insurers −122bn, banks −67bn — all five investor groups net sellers, −KRW 1.6tn in total. Beyond 10 years the total was −33bn, with only banks buying (+73bn). By line, offshore sold KRW 186bn of the new 5Y benchmark 26-9 and bought 191bn of 26-8. Every cash investor sold into the rally and only futures lifted the price — a level built that way is fragile to a pullback.

The auction was taken, and the shorts are not yet covered. The 3Y 26-5 stopped at 3.940% for KRW 2.4tn and closed at 3.918, 2.2bp through the stop. Borrow went 37,189 → 36,389bn (−800) and the share on loan 25.34 → 21.30% — exactly the dilution level (21.8%) flagged yesterday, with almost no real repayment. On the 26-7 precedent (auction 10/6, dilution 10/7, repayment 10/8), the repayment should show in today's data. The point is that KRW 3.6tn of shorts sat unchanged while the 3Y rallied 7.9bp — whoever built shorts into the auction is under water and has not covered, so the front-end covering fuel is still there. Going the other way, the 30Y benchmark 26-8 added KRW 91bn of borrow (1,501bn, 21.03%) — shorts went back on three sessions after the KRW 635bn repayment of 10/8, and that is why the 30Y could not keep up with the 10Y (−7.2 against −11.5). The old 5Y benchmark 26-3 added 85bn (2,454bn) and 26-9 30bn. Shorts are building in the 3Y basket and the 30Y, and have been cleared in the 2Y (26-7 at KRW 83bn).

Today is CPI eve, and the US was shut yesterday. The 10/9 US close was 2Y 4.790 (+2.8) · 5Y 5.018 (+2.9) · 10Y 5.241 (+1.4) · 30Y 5.5975 (−0.4) — Friday alone was a front-led bear flattening — and WTI is 93.74 (+2.1%), up 6% in three sessions from 88.28 on 10/8. With only Korea trading, KR10−US10 is −94.4bp (−12.9), a 60-day low (z −2.59). Closing sell-Korea-10Y / buy-US-10Y at −82.9 was right — anyone still in it is 11.5bp offside in one session. The frame for today — ① No fresh buying above 105.41. This is a level lifted by futures alone while every cash investor sold, so the pullback comes first. But the 26-5 shorts are uncovered and offshore 3Y buying is new length, so it is not a collapse either — trade the range. ② The 10-30 steepener is halfway there (4.5 → 8.8 against a 12–15 target); new entries only on a pullback to 5–6bp around the 50Y auction. ③ 3-10 at 37.9 is a 60-day z of −2.29 — if today's data confirms a 26-5 repayment the 3Y loses its own catalyst, and the 10/19 KRW 2.1tn 10Y auction is 10Y supply, so a 3-10 steepener (buy 3Y / sell 10Y) is the candidate. But the one-year z is −0.37, around average, so we do not act on the 60-day extreme alone — the lesson from 10-30. ④ 24-12 against 25-4 went the wrong way, to 6.0bp (−2.9). Hold into the 10/21 buyback, cut below the 3M low of 5.0.
