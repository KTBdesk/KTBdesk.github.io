03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Yesterday's long-end selling was the US during Korean hours, not the issuance plan. KTB 10Y +2.7 (4.442) · 30Y +2.2bp (4.552), futures 3Y −1 tick (102.36) · 10Y −32 ticks (104.12). A solid ISM print took the UST 10Y above 5.34% while Korea was open, and the retracement — fading ECB hike expectations, UST 2Y −9.8 and 10Y −4.5bp — came after the close. We expected little to happen before payrolls and got a bull steepener instead, and today's actual opens printed above the expected ones (3Y 102.46 · 10Y 104.41), right up at the top of the band (102.59 · 104.63). → Do not chase the rally today; work it in relative value.

The plan cut supply, and the only tenor that went up is the 20Y. October competitive auctions come to KRW 12tn against 16tn in September (the monthly programme was cut 5tn as well). By tenor: 2Y −1.0tn, 3Y −0.8tn, 5Y −0.8tn, 10Y −0.7tn, 30Y −0.7tn, 50Y −0.2tn, with only the 20Y up, by 0.1tn (linkers +0.1tn). In share terms the 30Y slipped from 15.6% to 15.0%, but the 20Y went from 3.8% to 5.8%. The KRW 3.5tn buyback on 10/21 takes 24-4·25-6·24-12·25-4·23-6·22-5·22-14·23-11 — 2 to 10-year off-the-runs only, nothing 20 years or longer — and the KRW 500bn exchange on 10/20 issues 26-8 (30Y) against 12-5·21-2·23-7·24-2·24-8. → The near-term catalyst sits in the 10-year off-the-runs on the buyback list (22-14 at 4.259, 23-11 at 4.335).

Cheap is the 10Y; expensive is the 5Y and 26-10. 3-10 at 42.7bp (+2.7) and 5-10 at 24.2 (+3.8, z +0.06, back to average) made the 10Y the cheapest point in a day, while 3-5 at 18.5bp is a 60-day low (z −2.32, but only −0.74 on one year). The −0.2bp inversion in 10-20 is one bond: 25-9 at 4.555 yields the same as the 30Y benchmark 26-8 (4.552), and the gap to 26-10 is 11.5bp (26-10 has KRW 842bn listed, 37.1% on loan). That scarcity breaks on 10/27 — a KRW 700bn 20Y auction lifts the listing to 1,542bn, up 83%. In futures the 10Y went from 2.1 ticks rich to 8.1 ticks cheap, so yesterday's case for selling it has gone. On flows, offshore dumped futures (10Y −7,582 lots), cash was flat at +KRW 20.2bn, and insurers and pensions have eased off the ultra-long.

The directional call waits for payrolls. Korea September CPI at 08:00, US September payrolls at 21:30 KST. The UST 2Y and KTB 3Y have both put in a chart trend, so if front-end delta is already on, riding that trend is defensible — but energy and commodities are coming out of their downward pressure (WTI $92.87, +2.71%), which cuts the other way. With US 2-5 at 22.0bp, a 60-day high (z +2.14), a strong print retraces a lot. Keep front-end delta but take it down in size. Money market: call 3.110 (−1.0) · CD91 3.210 (−1.0) · MSB 1Y 3.566% (+2.7).
