03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Yesterday offshore dumped futures. KTB 2Y +10.5 (4.070) · 3Y +11.6 (4.111) · 5Y +13.0 (4.330) · 10Y +15.7 (4.527) · 20Y +10.5 (4.558) · 30Y +10.5bp (4.655), futures 3Y −39 ticks (102.03) · 10Y −141 ticks (103.35). Offshore sold 18,016 3Y and 11,982 10Y contracts, undoing the pre-holiday buying in a single day. It was a gap to reprice the holiday move in US yields — but Korea did not follow all of it.

Expensive: 10Y and 20Y. Cheap: the front.
· The front is cheap — the 3Y at 4.111% is 111.1bp over the base rate (99.5bp on 09-23). 2-3 at 4.1bp and 2-5 at 26.0bp are still around −2σ, but the level has moved
· The 10Y is expensive — KR10−US10 at −71.0bp (60-day z −3.22), the widest inversion, with the UST 10Y at 5.2375% against a KTB 10Y of 4.527%
· The 20Y is rich again — 10-20 compressed to 3.1bp (−5.2) and the 10-20-30 fly went 1.4 → 6.6bp (+1.94σ), on insurers buying KRW 272.9bn of ultra-longs
· The 30Y is the cheaper of the two — 10-30 flattened 5.2bp to 12.8bp while 20-30 held at 9.7bp (+1.28σ)

The US sold off again yesterday. 2Y +8.0bp (4.932) · 5Y +8.4 (5.0715) · 10Y +7.7 (5.2375) · 30Y +5.8bp (5.5485), another leg above Friday's close. The curve kept its holiday steepening (2-10 30.5bp) while the long end stays compressed — 20-30 −5.5bp (z60 −2.51) and 10-30 31.1bp (one-year z −3.27). The UST 2Y at 4.932% is 18bp above the 4.75% floor we work with.

Non-policy pressure is building on US long rates. Deficit-driven Treasury supply and heavy big-tech issuance are together pushing the term premium up. Overnight the 2Y went back above 4.9%, the 10Y above 5.2% and the 30Y above 5.5%. Even if Fed hike expectations ease, the 10Y and 30Y cannot fall as quickly as they used to while supply pressure remains — and with long rates now rising alongside short rates, the set of tenors that avoids volatility keeps shrinking.

Local real money absorbed it. Offshore sold only KRW 299.8bn of cash KTBs (≤10Y −199.5bn, >10Y −62.9bn) against pensions +972.4bn, insurers +284.8bn (ultra-long +272.9bn), trusts +259.8bn and banks +190.1bn. What offshore sold in futures, domestic institutions took in cash. On the week offshore is still a seller of the ultra-long, at −KRW 1.12tn.

The futures discount widened. Versus fair value the 3Y is 7.1 ticks cheap (102.101) and the 10Y 12.2 ticks cheap (103.472), with market basis at −29.5 and −42.1 ticks. Implied − cash also widened to 3Y +16.1bp (60-day +1.38σ) and 10Y +5.2bp (+1.68σ). The dislocation exists because offshore sold futures only.

Today — the RBA is the gate. As set out in yesterday's close note, the first clip at the front is on and the second waits for the RBA; the stop on the 3Y contract stays 101.80. Tomorrow brings US August PCE (21:30) on month and quarter end, and Thursday the October KTB issuance plan. With the PD meeting cancelled there is no advance read on ultra-long supply, so 10-30 volatility into Thursday can be high. The 10Y, inverted 71bp to the US, is not something to own outright.

Funding and policy. Call 3.050% · CD91 3.220% · MSB 1Y 3.498%, with CD higher for a second day. 1Y swap − base 77.7bp (+6.5); 3Y − base 111.1bp (+11.6).
