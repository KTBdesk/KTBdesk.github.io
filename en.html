02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

This time it was the belly that gave way. KTB yields rose 2Y +4.0bp (3.760%), 3Y +5.6bp (3.935%), 5Y +6.0bp (4.170%), 10Y +4.7bp (4.417%), 20Y +6.2bp (4.622%) and 30Y +4.0bp (4.667%). Where the ultra-long end had jumped 12.4bp on its own the day before, this move was led by the 3-5 year sector: 2-5 widened 2.0bp to 41.0bp while 10-30 came in 0.7bp to 25.0bp and 20-30 2.2bp to 4.5bp. That bonds could not rally on a day the KOSPI fell 3.99% tells you what kind of market this is.

Overnight the US went up and then gave it back. The 10Y traded above 4.8% early on the back of crude, then turned lower as limited wage pressure alongside a soft employment print brought growth concerns back into focus. It closed 2Y −3.1bp (4.371%), 5Y −2.2bp (4.535%), 10Y −1.9bp (4.780%) and 30Y −1.6bp (5.259%) — a bull steepening, with the front end down most. WTI at 91.01 (+0.88%) made it four sessions higher.

The September hike is still very much in the price. On CME pricing, the probability of a September hike remains above 60%. If tomorrow's US August payrolls print firm, the front end gets pushed again — and the fact that the 2Y only retraced 3.1bp after all of that says how little cushion there is.

The ultra-long end has moved from a supply story to a demand test. Foreign buying of +2,400 in 26-8 is best read as settlement of the 30Y auction rather than new demand; on the day they actually reduced off-the-run ultra-longs, selling 400 of 24-2 and 387 of 23-7, and added nothing to 25-9, which they had been accumulating steadily. Insurers and pensions went the other way, taking +1,205 in 26-2 and +310 in 26-8, plus +1,300 in the 10Y on-the-run 26-6 and +1,000 in 25-11 — a visibly firmer stance since the start of September.

Which puts the next move in 10-30 in foreign hands. Reduced 30Y issuance, buybacks and the WGBI inflow have largely been digested. Insurance and pension demand alone is not yet large enough, so any further tightening in 10-30 needs offshore accounts to show up again at the long end.
