The 2Y auction: KRW 1.8tn awarded at 3.725 — a new barrier that worked the same afternoon. The award was 2bp above Friday's 3.705 mark; the 2Y traded up to 3.728 and then closed at 3.715, back through the award — the best-performing tenor on the day (+1.0). Hence 2-3 2.5 (+2.0) · 2-5 18.2 (+3.2), both jumping off their 60-day lows (0.5 · 15.0) in one session. Borrow in 26-7 fell 79bn on 134bn of repayments (394bn · 4.13%) — nearly half of the 370bn short built into the auction was covered after it. 26-7 vs 25-10 moved from −3.8 to −2.5 (z −2.36 → −1.66), so the 2Y benchmark's richness eased too. The previous barrier, 3.875 from 10/6, is 16bp away and irrelevant for now; from today the front end is a buy above 3.725 and a hold below it.

Against the Asian close, though, US yields fell only a little, and overnight Oracle and Meta announced further funding plans, a supply weight that keeps the long end from following oil all the way. The risk for rates is a Republican outperformance (tax cuts and spending momentum intact, supply worries back); a divided government would not by itself shrink the deficit and supply already in place.
