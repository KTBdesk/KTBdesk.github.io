03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

The 10Y got through its auction cleanly. It touched 5.365% and the 30Y 5.73%, the highest since 2002, then gave it all back to close at 5.2845% once the auction stopped at 5.30% — through the market — with a 2.77x bid-to-cover against a 2.54x average over the last six. Cheapening into supply and recovering once it is absorbed is the classic shape, and it confirms once that the place where supply gets taken down is also where the long end gets covered. Tonight's 30Y (02:00, 5.308% last) is the second test.

And again only the front came down. The 2Y at 4.7685 (−3.0bp) has broken below its 5, 20, 60, 120 and 200-day moving averages and sits on the lower Bollinger band. Meanwhile the 10Y at 5.2845 (+0.2), the 20Y at 5.712 (+1.0) and the 30Y at 5.6725 (+1.3) left the 20Y and 30Y at series highs (one-year z +3.13 and +3.20). 2-10 at 51.6bp (+3.25) is just under its 60-day high of 53.35, and 2-5 at 25.85bp is the 60-day high itself (z +2.70).

The 5Y is the cheapest point against its wings. The 2-5-10 fly at −0.10bp carries z60 −1.69 and one-year z −2.55, just above its 60-day low of −0.75. With a December hike fully priced, the 2Y can only go further if that December pricing comes out — and that is a question only the CPI answers. Buying the front here is poor asymmetry.

The IEA capped oil. Brent traded back above 100 intraday on supply-disruption worries, but the IEA brought forward reserve releases and prioritised diesel supply, taking Brent back to around 100 and WTI below 90. Aiming directly at refined products takes some of the upside risk out of the ISM prices-paid story.

Into next week's inflation prints, cut direction and trade relative value. CPI 10/14, PPI 10/15, euro area CPI 10/16. In the US the 2-5-10 fly beats 2-5 outright — it expresses the same "the 5Y is cheap" view while carrying half the exposure to the 2Y, which moves first on a soft CPI. Read the euro area print together with French fiscal: OAT-Bund narrowed to 127.4bp on Le Pen's shadow budget, but a high euro area number raises the pressure on the ECB and widens peripheral spreads again — and that rotation into the liquid core is a bullish input for the US 10Y.
