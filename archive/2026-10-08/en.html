03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Yesterday the front cheapened and only the ultra-long rallied. Cash KTBs went 2Y +2.7 (3.898) · 3Y +2.8 (3.962) · 5Y +1.5 (4.132) · 10Y +0.3 (4.375) · 20Y +0.8bp (4.413) while the 30Y fell 3.5bp to 4.485 and the 50Y 3.5bp to 4.450. Futures closed 3Y −6 ticks (102.57) and 10Y −4 ticks (104.64). 10-30 went from 14.8 to 11.0bp, a 60-day low, with 20-30 at 7.2 (−4.3) and 5-30 at 35.3 (−5.0). At the front, 3-5 at 17.0bp is also a 60-day low (z −2.60).

The 30Y auction's answer was demand. In the cash market insurers bought KRW 711bn and offshore KRW 384bn beyond 10 years, with banks +144bn, pensions +59bn and asset managers +41bn — KRW 1,339bn in all. That is the exact opposite of the day before, when offshore sold KRW 200bn of ultra-long paper. At the same time the new issue took 26-8's listed amount from KRW 5.07tn to 6.87tn, diluting the share out on loan from 40.58% to 29.93% — the constraint that there was nothing left to borrow has been lifted, and with it much of the short-covering fuel.

Overnight the US front end fell again. 2Y 4.7685 (−3.0) · 5Y 5.027 (−0.8) · 10Y 5.2845 (+0.2) · 30Y 5.6725 (+1.3). The 10Y touched 5.365% and the 30Y 5.73%, the highest since 2002, before retracing: the 10Y auction stopped at 5.30%, through the market at the time, with a 2.77x bid-to-cover against a 2.54x average over the last six — absorbing supply is where the long end gets covered, now confirmed in the numbers, with tonight's 30Y the second test. The September FOMC minutes had most participants seeing one more hike by year-end as likely appropriate while stressing a meeting-by-meeting call, and October odds fell to 17.2%. And yet 2-5 at 25.85bp is a 60-day high (z +2.70), the 2-5-10 fly at −0.10 carries a one-year z of −2.55, and the 20Y at 5.712% and 30Y at 5.6725% are series highs.

Into next week's inflation prints (US CPI 10/14, PPI 10/15, euro area CPI 10/16), cut direction and trade relative value. ① In the US the 5Y is the cheapest point, but the 2-5-10 fly beats 2-5 outright — with a December hike fully priced, a soft CPI moves the 2Y first, and the fly carries half the 2Y exposure. ② Do not buy the Korean front — the 3Y is back to 96.2bp over base but still under 100bp, with no fresh catalyst before the 10/22 BOK. ③ Do not chase 10-30 lower from 11.0bp — the borrow constraint is gone and the 50Y on 10/16, the 10/20 exchange (which issues 26-8) and the 20Y increase on 10/27 are all still to come. ④ Cut delta before today's close — the 10/9 holiday means two sessions of US news, the 30Y auction included, arrive together on 10/12.

All five accounts bought beyond 10 years, but strip out the auction and it reads differently. Of the KRW 1,339bn total, the 26-8 allocation accounted for 1,130bn — offshore 493bn, insurers and pensions 584bn, asset managers 53bn, about 63% of the KRW 1.8tn on offer. Excluding that allocation, offshore were net sellers of KRW 170bn of off-the-run ultra-long paper (26-2 −58bn, 25-2 −41bn, 24-2 −40bn, 24-8 −21bn). Insurers and pensions, by contrast, added 26-2 +87bn and 24-2 +36bn on top and bought strips, so the quality of real demand was clearly better on that side. Even so, September's 30Y auction saw 54.4% taken down (KRW 1,360bn of 2.5tn); this month's 63% mostly reflects KRW 700bn less competitive supply, and the absolute size of real demand actually fell. And with much of the insurer bid since September concentrated in a single company, it is too early to call this a sector-wide return.
