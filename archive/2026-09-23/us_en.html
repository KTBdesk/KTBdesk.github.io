03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Oil fell again and yields still closed slightly higher. 2Y +0.5bp (4.7545%) · 5Y +1.0bp (4.8365%) · 10Y +1.1bp (4.9635%) · 20Y unchanged (5.328%) · 30Y +1.4bp (5.2995%). The 2Y was more than 5bp lower intraday before retracing to about 4.75% — the front end could not keep the benefit of cheaper oil.

The oil move is a supply story. Saudi Arabia restarted the East-West pipeline and is preparing to resume crude exports through Yanbu on the Red Sea, easing disruption fears, while Iran raised the possibility of reopening the Strait of Hormuz if US military pressure eases. WTI is below $90, a full reversal from the triple digits of 09/15.

The Fed path is unchanged. October hike odds 55%, at least one more by year-end 91%. Lower oil pulls breakevens and the term premium down, which helps the long end, but 2–5Y still carries the hike path and cannot rally on oil alone. This week's 2/5/7Y auctions are the test of that.

The curve barely moved, still near −2σ. 2-10 20.9bp (+0.6, −2.44σ) · 2-5 8.2bp (−2.29σ) · 5-10 12.7bp (−2.14σ) · 10-30 33.6bp (−1.95σ), with 20-30 still inverted at −2.9bp. On a one-year window the long end is the more extreme: 10-20 −3.64σ and 10-30 −3.19σ.

The gap to Korea is −51.6bp. Korea's front end rallied up to 2.5bp yesterday while the US front stood still, so the spread widened again. The 5Y and 7Y auctions and oil trade through Korea's holiday, and it all lands on Monday 09/28.
