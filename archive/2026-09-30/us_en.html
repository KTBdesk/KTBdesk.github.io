03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Only the front retraced. On 09-29: 2Y −5.5bp (4.8775%) · 5Y −2.2 (5.0495%) · 10Y −0.1 (5.2365%) · 20Y +1.8 (5.621%) · 30Y +1.9bp (5.5675%). The 2Y dropped back below 4.9% while the 20Y and 30Y cheapened further.

The result is a steepening. 2-10 35.9bp (+5.4) · 2-5 17.2 (+3.2) · 5-10 18.7 (+2.1), led by the front. The long end did not join: 10-30 33.1bp (one-year z −2.95) · 10-20 38.5 (−2.95σ) · 20-30 −5.4bp (z60 −2.27) — fiscal and supply pressure still sits only there.

The 5Y is the cheapest point. The 2-5-10 fly at 1.5bp is −1.80σ on 60 days and −2.55σ on one year, near its one-year low. It is the tenor that responds first when the front retraces, while 10Y and longer stay tied to supply — so the fly (long 5Y, short 2Y and 10Y) is the cleanest expression.

Tonight's PCE is the hinge. A benign August core print (3.3% y/y prior) puts the 2Y back on the path to the 4.75% floor; a firm one reopens 5%. Month and quarter end falls the same day, bringing index duration extension with it, and Friday's September payrolls completes the picture for October.
