03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Korea rallied yesterday even as US yields rose. KTB 2Y −4.0 (4.030) · 3Y −3.9 (4.072) · 5Y −6.6 (4.264) · 10Y −5.7 (4.470) · 20Y −4.8 (4.510) · 30Y −6.3bp (4.592), futures 3Y +16 ticks (102.19) · 10Y +51 ticks (103.86). In the morning, with the 3Y benchmark more than 110bp over the base rate, bargain buying and short covering came in; in the afternoon the RBA hiked 25bp to 4.60% and, with Bullock's press conference read dovish, the Australian 3Y fell 8bp and Korea extended the rally alongside it. It was the RBA's fourth hike this year, the highest rate since 2011, and unanimous.

Offshore futures did the lifting. 3Y +7,300 lots, undoing nearly half of the previous day's −18,016, with securities (−7,488) on the other side; the 10Y was −1,656, so the two contracts split. Oil helped — WTI at $89.38 (−3.5%) lost the $90 handle — and USD/KRW fell 8.4 won to 1,356.70.

But nobody bought cash. Offshore sold KRW 591.3bn of KTBs, pensions 459.8bn, insurers 195.8bn and banks 79.3bn — every major account was a net seller and yields still fell. Offshore cut short off-the-runs (16-8 −61.4 · 22-1 −100.0 · 24-4 −50.5 · 25-6 −128.2bn) and, around the 5Y, 25-3 −146.6 and 26-3 −28.5bn; the ultra-long was −110bn (25-9 −29.4 · 25-7 −42.4 · 26-2 −94.8 · 26-8 −26.2bn). Insurers and pensions also sold the ultra-long — 23-2 −51.0, 24-2 −20.0, 26-2 −73.0bn, more than 100bn net even after +30bn of 26-2 strips. A rally built on short covering rather than cash demand is vulnerable to a reversal.

The US retraced only at the front. 2Y −5.5bp (4.8775) · 5Y −2.2 (5.0495) · 10Y −0.1 (5.2365) · 20Y +1.8 (5.621) · 30Y +1.9bp (5.5675), so 2-10 widened to 35.9bp (+5.4) while 10-30 33.1bp (one-year z −2.95) and 20-30 −5.4bp (z60 −2.27) stayed compressed — the supply burden still sits only at the long end.

Expensive: the KTB 10Y. Cheap: the UST 5Y.
· KR10−US10 at −76.7bp (60-day z −3.36) — 5.6bp wider in a day and a fresh record inversion. Not a level at which to own the KTB 10Y outright
· The US 2-5-10 fly at 1.5bp (60-day −1.80σ, one-year −2.55σ) — the 5Y is the cheapest point against its wings
· In Korea 2-5 23.4bp (−2.53σ) and 3-5 19.2 (−2.22σ) are compressed, while 20-30 8.2bp (+0.84σ) and the 10-20-30 fly 4.2 (+1.65σ) keep the 20Y rich

The futures discount survived the rally. Versus fair value the 3Y is 4.8 ticks cheap (102.238) and the 10Y 9.2 ticks cheap (103.952), with market basis at −26.1 and −37.6 ticks. The 10Y contract gained 51 ticks and is still 9 ticks cheap, which says cash was stronger still.

Today and tomorrow decide the week. US August PCE lands tonight (21:30 KST) on month and quarter end, and the October KTB issuance plan comes tomorrow. With the PD meeting cancelled there is no advance read on ultra-long supply, and with offshore and now real money both selling the ultra-long, an increase in long-end issuance would combine weaker demand with more supply — a steepening risk at the back. On the other side, today's month-end WGBI index flows are the offset to watch. Money market: call 3.110 (+2.0) · CD91 3.220 · MSB 1Y 3.528% (+3.0).
