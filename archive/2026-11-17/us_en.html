04 Macro Issues & Curve Dislocations

A tech sell-off — yields fell only a little. Doubts over the durability of AI spending and caution ahead of Nvidia's results took semiconductor shares down more than 5% and Nvidia more than 4%. S&P 500 −1.92% · Nasdaq −2.49% · Dow −1.41%. 11/16 closes 2Y 4.833 · 5Y 4.941 · 10Y 5.132 · 20Y 5.524 · 30Y 5.476 — 2Y −1.3bp · 5Y −2.2bp · 10Y −0.7bp · 30Y +0.8bp. → With inflation still a concern and a further December hike still expected, the fall in yields was small next to the fall in equities.

Oil — from a supply shortfall to a forecast surplus. WTI 85.72 (−3.2%) · Brent 92.44 (−3.6%). Middle East crude exports through the Strait of Hormuz recovered quickly — dated Brent, at 136 dollars in early October, is near 93. The IEA's November report put October world supply at 105.4mn barrels a day (+2.3mn) and raised its fourth-quarter supply forecast by 3.3mn. Demand this year is seen 2.7mn lower than last year — supply exceeds demand by 1.7mn in the fourth quarter. Most of October's stock build (+52mn barrels) was oil on water, though; onshore stocks are the lowest since 2016 and the shortage of refined products such as diesel is unchanged. → Lower crude may be slow to reach consumer prices.

View — the weaker-bond view is kept until the 11/19 listing, the buy zone after it. Since the 11/5 close-out levels (2Y 4.463 · 10Y 4.837) the 2Y is +37bp and the 10Y +30bp. The 60-day highs are 4.932 · 5.288. Curve: 2-10 29.9 (+0.6) · 5-10 19.1 (+1.6) · 10-30 34.4 (+1.4) — the 5Y fell the most. KR10−US10 −115.9 (−1.8). Fed's Hammack spoke of a preference for gradual hikes while also raising a larger step, and the ECB's Vujčić said a hike next year is highly likely — rather than moving in opposite directions, equities and bonds may be heading back to a regime where both move together on inflation. That conclusion needs more time. → Nothing is chased lower, and the time to look at buying again will be flagged separately. Today 2Y 4.7813~4.91 · 10Y 5.06~5.1958.
