02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

It printed 4.8% and then handed it back. Early on 09-02 the 10Y traded above 4.8% on the back of crude, before turning lower as limited wage pressure alongside a soft employment print put growth concerns back on the table. The close was 2Y −3.1bp (4.371%), 5Y −2.2bp (4.535%), 10Y −1.9bp (4.780%) and 30Y −1.6bp (5.259%) — a bull steepening led by the front end.

The retracement was small. After two sessions in which the 2Y rose 5.4bp and then 11.3bp, giving back only 3.1bp says the hawkish pricing built into the front end does not unwind on a single data point. On CME pricing, a September hike is still above 60%.

Crude keeps pointing up. WTI at 91.01 (+0.88%) made it four sessions higher, +10.9% cumulatively. While Middle East supply risk persists, term premium keeps a floor under long-end yields and caps how far any rally can go. Gold went the other way at 4,396.40 (−1.90%), a third consecutive decline.

The steepener setup still stands. 5-10 (24.5bp, −0.79σ), 10-20 (48.4bp, −1.04σ) and 10-30 (47.9bp, −0.87σ) all sit below 1σ. 2-5, by contrast, is at 16.5bp and +0.95σ — already wide inside the front end. A firm payrolls print tomorrow would naturally compress 2-5 while widening 10-20 and 10-30.
