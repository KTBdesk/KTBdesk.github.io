03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

On 10/8 the front cheapened again and only the 30Y rallied. Cash KTBs went 2Y +1.2 (3.910) · 3Y +3.5 (3.997) · 5Y +4.2 (4.174) · 10Y +3.7 (4.412) · 20Y +0.3 (4.416) while the 30Y fell 2.8bp to 4.457 and the 50Y 2.7bp. Futures closed on their lows at 102.50 (−7 ticks) and 104.40 (−24). 10-30 went from 11.0 to 4.5bp, a 60-day low, 10-20 is down to 0.4bp — the 10Y and 20Y are effectively the same bond — and 5-30 fell to 28.3 (−7.0). The 3Y, meanwhile, is back to 99.7bp over base, just short of 100.

But that 30Y rally was short covering, not real demand. Cash buying beyond 10 years was −KRW 53bn on the day: only insurers bought (+102bn), against pensions −84bn and offshore −43bn. What moved was borrow — KRW 635bn of 26-8 was repaid in a single session (2,055 to 1,410bn), and the 2Y benchmark 26-7 emptied out by KRW 622bn (717 to 95bn). 26-8's share on loan has gone 40.58% to 29.93% to 20.42%, but the two steps are different things — the first is pure dilution, with the balance down only KRW 1bn while issuance took the listed amount from 5.07 to 6.87tn, and only the second is a real repayment, 2,055 to 1,410bn. The actual covering all happened on 10/8. By line it is clearer still: offshore bought KRW 148bn of 26-8 but sold KRW 190bn of everything else ultra-long (24-2 −70bn, 26-2 −57bn, 21-2 −30bn), and insurers and pensions actually sold 26-8, by KRW 47bn. The move came from stopped-out steepeners covering, and 26-8 now trades 2.3bp through 26-2. The same session saw KRW 622bn repaid in the 2Y benchmark 26-7 — shorts unwinding at the front and the ultra-long on the same day says whole curve positions were closed out at once.

While Korea was shut, the US long end rallied hard. Over two sessions: 2Y +2.2 (4.790) · 5Y −1.0 (5.0175) · 10Y −4.4 (5.241) · 20Y −6.6 (5.6455) · 30Y −7.5bp (5.5975). The 30Y auction cleared just as the 10Y had. 2-10 compressed from 53.2bp intraday on 10/8 to 45.1, so the crowded steepener really did unwind, and KR10−US10 narrowed from −91.1 to −82.9bp. But Michigan sentiment came in at 46.3 against 47.8 expected while one-year inflation expectations rose from 4.6% to 4.7%, and WTI is back to 91.85 from 88.28 — the IEA measures faded within three sessions. October hike odds are 19%.

This week has a clear buy list and a clear avoid list. Buy — ① today's 3Y auction: 26-5 is 25.34% on loan (+KRW 174bn) with shorts built right into it, and KRW 2.4tn of issuance takes the listed amount from 14.7 to 17.1tn, diluting that share to 21.8% — the same pattern already seen twice this month in 26-7 and 26-8. ② 24-12, 8.9bp over 25-4 against a 3M average of 12.4, with the buyback next week. Avoid — ① chasing 10-30 lower: the fuel is spent and Friday's KRW 600bn 50Y is the next test. ② adding delta before CPI (10/14) and PPI (10/15), with oil back up. ③ new US curve trades — the 2-10 flattening has already delivered 8bp.
