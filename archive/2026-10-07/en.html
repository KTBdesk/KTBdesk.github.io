03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Only the front of the Korean curve rallied yesterday. The 2Y fell 1.5bp to 3.871 and everything else backed up — 3Y +0.7 (3.934) · 5Y +0.2 (4.117) · 10Y +1.5 (4.372) · 20Y +3.0 (4.405) · 30Y +1.8bp (4.520). Futures closed 3Y −1 tick (102.63) and 10Y −16 ticks (104.68). The point is that the 2Y richened while absorbing its own KRW 2.1tn auction, taking 2-3 out to 6.3bp (+2.2). The worst performer was the 20Y (+3.0), so 10-20 steepened to 3.3bp (+1.5) while 20-30 compressed to 11.5 (−1.2). 3-5 at 18.3bp is a 60-day low (z −2.22) — the 5Y is still the most expensive point on the curve.

Overnight the US rallied on Europe, not on anything American. France put forward a tougher fiscal consolidation than the market expected, French yields fell sharply, and the move travelled through the euro area into Treasuries. Oil holding flat (WTI 89.44) kept inflation worries from building further. 2Y 4.800 (−1.4) · 5Y 5.036 (−2.3) · 10Y 5.284 (−2.2) · 30Y 5.661 (−0.3), with the 10Y trading roughly 3bp below its Asian-session level. And again only the front came down — 2-10 compressed 0.95bp while 10-30 widened 1.95 and 10-20 1.45, leaving the 20Y at 5.702% and the 30Y at 5.660%, both series highs. That a fiscal tightening in Europe was the good news is telling.

Today's 30Y auction is the fork in the road. Supply is helpful — October competitive issuance was cut from KRW 16tn to 12tn and the 30Y share keeps falling, from 20% in June to 15.0% in October. Demand is the problem. Offshore sold KRW 797bn of cash KTBs yesterday (KRW 200bn of it ultra-long) and their holdings fell KRW 0.9tn to 344.8tn. By line, 26-2 −167bn, 25-7 −35bn and 26-10 −15bn against just +24bn of 26-8. Insurers and pensions did buy a little at the ultra-long (about +30bn of 26-2), but the intensity is clearly down from the steady bid that held September together. And with 40.58% of 26-8 and 44.54% of 26-10 out on loan, there is nothing left to borrow. Watch the bid-to-cover and whether real money buys intraday, not just the stop-out yield.

So hold off on new 10-30 steepeners until the auction is done. It sits at 14.8bp (+0.3 · z60 −1.14 · one-year z +0.92). 26-2 against 26-8 closed 1.2bp inverted, but yesterday's intraday switches went through around 2bp inverted — if the auction clears cleanly and real-money buying returns, the inversion goes back out to those levels and 10-30 comes under flattening pressure. If the bid-to-cover disappoints and no real demand shows up, the steepener works. Either way the call belongs after 11:00, not before. For context, 26-2 has already cheapened on KRW 167bn of offshore selling plus a KRW 683bn repayment of borrowed stock, while 26-8's only available supply is the auction itself — two 30Y lines with opposite supply conditions, which is the premise for reading the inversion.
