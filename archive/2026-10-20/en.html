Korea +4.5 against the US +6.55 took KR10−US10 to −93 (−2.05). Today's US 10Y range is 5.08~5.191 — above 5.19 the Korean 10Y contract tests its range bottom (105.46).

BOK preview — a hold is a given; the issues are the growth language and November. Fourteen of fifteen economists in a local survey expect a November hike; domestic accounts lean to a hold and differ only on a dissent. Under Governor Shin the dot plot and reaction-function communication have cut the signal value of a dissent, so even a unanimous hold leaves the November call intact. The swing factor is the growth language — the deputy prime minister cited 3.4–3.6% for this year against the BOK's August 3.3%, and July's statement carried a "well above" line, so an upside acknowledgement is expected again. The BOK report released on meeting day, on the conditions for a private-consumption recovery, asks whether the export boom spreads to domestic demand. A hawkish read would unwind part of the offshore futures buying that came in before the meeting and cap further yield declines — that is why A is halved before Wednesday's close. The case for a hold (core m/m slowing, no clear demand-side pressure, growth concentrated in semiconductors) is adequate, but an October hold is not yet a slowdown of the cycle.

Strategy — bullish into the BOK, 10Y-led, but now with conditions. ① The 10Y long (mock book A, 140 contracts entered at today's 105.95 open) carries a 105.57 stop; nothing added until 105.82 holds. ② The 2-3 steepener (book B) goes on today at 4.7 — pre-positioning for the pattern in which the 2Y beat the 3Y after both hold-after-two-hikes precedents (Feb 2022, Feb 2023). ③ 30Y: KRW 3bn of 26-8 at 4.30~4.32 after today's exchange (book C) — offshore 26-8 +767bn in October and insurer allocations have absorbed supply every time. ④ Buyback lines (23-6, 22-5, 24-4) are fully borrowed; expect covering after tomorrow's tender. 24-12 vs 25-4 at 8.5 (+2.6) is that effect — no re-entry. ⑤ Credit: 1Y spreads keep widening, no added exposure.
