02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Sharp flattening — only the ultra-long end rallied. On 08-20 the KTB 2Y rose 3.7bp and the 3Y 3.5bp, while the 30Y fell 5.8bp to 4.672% and the 20Y 2.8bp, collapsing 3-30 by 9.3bp and 10-30 by 4.5bp. Futures split: 3Y −0.08 (103.22), 10Y +0.13 (105.57). Foreigners flipped to −8,364 in 3Y futures after +14,083 the day before, with securities firms (+7,102) and banks (+1,162) absorbing. KOSPI rebounded +5.9% and USD/KRW fell to 1,392.6 (−5.1).

The US moved the other way — weaker and steeper. Overnight 10Y +6.0bp (4.707%), 30Y +5.7bp (5.249%), 2Y +2.6bp gave back most of the post-auction rally, widening 2-10 to 51.7bp (+3.4bp). Caution ahead of PCE (8/26) and Jackson Hole (8/27~29) is back in focus.

Yesterday's flattener worked within a day. 10-30 went 39.5→35.0bp and 20-30 10.0→7.0bp, close to the first targets. RV ranks 1 and 2 remain the 10-30 and 20-30 flatteners (86 each) — targets 10-30 29.2bp (full mean 16.5) and 20-30 5.4bp. Rank 3 is the 3-10-30 butterfly (79.7), while the tactical 10Y short (45.5) and the 3-10 steepener (32.9) stay flagged "avoid — no new entries".
