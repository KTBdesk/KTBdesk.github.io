02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

On Monday the Korean 10Y sold off on its own. 10Y +4.0bp (4.390%) was the weakest point, 5Y +3.0bp (4.125%) next, while 2Y +1.6bp (3.721%), 3Y +1.2bp (3.897%), 20Y +0.7bp (4.577%) and 30Y +0.2bp (4.639%) barely moved. So 3-10 widened 2.8bp to 49.3 while 10-30 narrowed 3.8bp to 24.9 and 10-20 3.3bp to 18.7 — the curve kinked around the 10Y from both sides. 3-10 is at a five-session high and the 94.8th percentile of the past 250 days; 5-10 at 26.5bp, +1.08σ, is back at the top of the KTB curve. Futures: 3Y −5 ticks (103.12), 10Y −27 ticks (105.17), with 3Y volume jumping to 166k.

The 3Y auction cleared strongly. KRW 3.2tn of 26-5 was awarded at 3.875%, 1bp through the prior close (3.885), and 26-5 finished at 3.897 (+1.2bp), holding up better than the 5Y and 10Y. The pre-auction concession flagged yesterday unwound about halfway: vs 25-10 from 9.5 to 8.8bp, vs 26-3 from −0.85σ to −0.08σ. With the front end settled, the weight of the sell-off migrated to the 10Y.

The US was closed; Europe and Asia sold first. Treasuries sit at Friday's closes (2Y 4.368, 10Y 4.778) for Labor Day, but on Monday Bunds +4.6bp (3.400%), gilts +4.6bp (5.106%), Australia +3.0bp (5.190%). WTI 92.50 (+1.11%), a seventh straight gain, has settled above $90, and KOSPI +4.61% (6,995) showed strong risk appetite. USD/JPY 154.27 (−1.42%) — yen strength returned — and USD/KRW 1,340.50 (−0.74%). A pair that was above 160 as recently as last week is now below 155 on BOJ tightening expectations and carry-unwind worries; the speed of the yen is the kind of variable that forces global position adjustments, so bonds have to watch it too. Tonight is the first US session after the holiday, so all of this gets priced at once.

Inside the front end the story is funding, not rates. On Monday sub-1Y swaps and cash underperformed every other tenor, and credit saw selling build quickly. This week stacks up the reserve-maintenance date, KTB principal redemptions, withholding-tax payments and a ~KRW 37tn BOK RP maturity, so the sub-1Y sector is responding to liquidity conditions before rate direction. Half the reason the 3Y contract could not rally on a strong auction sits here.

Offshore bought the 3Y contract in size, and the other side was the banks. Futures: 3Y +15,015, 10Y +2,635. The same day banks sold 12,900 3Y contracts — the classic auction-day pattern of the allottees hedging in futures and offshore taking the other side. Cumulative 3Y at 65,647 re-overtook the 10Y (61,460) within a day. In cash, offshore turned buyer at +KRW 115.1bn, led by 26-2 +142.1bn — buying back almost exactly Friday's −114.9bn sale, which strengthens the bond-forward-unwind reading. 25-9 was bought again (+38.0bn) and the new auction paper 26-5 (+50.1bn). Against that, the 10Y benchmark 26-6 was sold (−39.0bn) — consistent with the 10Y underperformance.

Today — preliminary 2Q GDP at 08:00 (advance YoY 3.7%, QoQ 0.6%), then the US catches up on three days overnight. Given Europe +4.6bp, crude at 92.5 and a stronger yen, higher US yields at the open are the base case. Korea's front end has had its auction relief, but the 10Y carries the 26-6 selling, a premium still unwinding (8 → 4 ticks) and the European move. Five sessions to 10Y expiry. Yesterday's "Korea leans flatter" was wrong on 3-10 within a day and right on 10-30 — this is a market whose weight sits on the 10Y, which resists a simple front-or-back framing.

The cheap bond changed from 26-5 to 25-4 — or more precisely, 25-4 was the only one that did not sell off. Of the three basket bonds, 25-4 (2028.06) rose just 0.1bp against 25-10 +1.9 and 26-3 +3.0, taking 25-4 vs 25-10 to +1.70σ, vs 26-5 to +1.86σ, vs 26-3 to +1.21σ — the shortest bond in the basket is now the richest against everything, at three-month extremes. 26-5, with the auction clearing 1bp through the screen, unwound about half of its concession: vs 25-10 from 9.5 to 8.8bp, vs 26-3 from −0.85σ to −0.08σ. For the roll — 25-4 is not in the December basket (25-10, 26-5, 26-3). Yesterday's 11-tick Dec discount (102.68 vs 102.79) was the evidence; the favourable roll (sell Sep, buy Dec) lasts until the 25-4 richness unwinds.
