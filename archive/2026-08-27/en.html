02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Long end led the rally into the BOK — only the front end lagged. On 08-26 the KTB 10Y fell 3.6bp to 4.282%, the 20Y 3.3bp to 4.542% and the 30Y 3.7bp to 4.592%, while the 2Y rose 1.0bp to 3.700% on its own. Futures followed with 3Y +0.03 (103.32) and 10Y +0.29 (105.94). The drivers were lower oil on reduced US-Iran escalation risk and foreign buying of futures. With calls for a hike and a hold evenly split, however, the rally faded through the afternoon.

The foreign 3Y cumulative position set a new 6-month high. A +9,234 session took the cumulative to 133,199, above the prior 6-month peak of 131,180. On the other side, securities firms hit a 6-month low of −72,748 the same day — the two have pulled to opposite extremes. The 10Y leg bought a second day at +3,901.

The US sold off from the front. 2Y +3.7bp (4.212%), 10Y +1.8bp (4.647%), 30Y +0.2bp (5.169%) — a bear flattening that pulled 2-10 to 43.5bp (−1.9bp). US–KR 10Y widened back out to 36.5bp (+5.4bp) in a single session.

Curve richness is essentially spent. 5-30 at +1.03σ, 10-30 at +0.99σ and 5-10 at +0.97σ are all back around 1σ, and 3-10 at 46.5bp (−2.7bp) is a 5-day low with a z of +0.43σ. RV ranks 1 and 2 stay the 10-30 (81.6) and 20-30 (81.3) flatteners, but the scores have slipped from 86 to 81. Avoids: tactical 10Y short (42.1) and 3-10 steepener (32.2).
