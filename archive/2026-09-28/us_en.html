03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

A heavy four-day sell-off. Versus 09-22: 2Y +9.7bp (4.8516) · 5Y +15.1bp (4.9875) · 10Y +19.7bp (5.1604) · 30Y +19.1bp (5.4905). On 09-24 the 10Y reached 5.20% and the 30Y 5.48% before Friday retraced part of it. The drivers were firm activity data, inflation concern and expectations of further Fed tightening, with the 2/5/7Y auctions on top.

The shape was a bear steepening. 2-10 20.9 → 30.9bp (+10.0) · 5-10 12.7 → 17.3bp (+4.6) · 2-5 8.2 → 13.6bp (+5.4). A curve pinned near −2σ all month has unwound to a 2-10 z60 of −1.10 (one-year z −1.70) in a week. 10-30 is unchanged at 33.0bp (z60 −1.68, one-year −3.00). The 20Y at 5.548 puts 10-20 at 38.8bp and 20-30 at −5.8bp, a deeper inversion (20-30 z60 −2.19) — the room left to normalise is still at the long end.

Oil moved the other way. US–Iran talks progressed from contact to working level, with reports of a constructive tone from Tehran. Supply-disruption fears eased further and WTI fell back below $100 to the low $90s. That produced a retracement in short rates, but not enough to undo the holiday move.

We treat 4.75% as the floor for the 2Y. At 4.85% it is a level worth owning, but with the Fed tightening watch alive it is too early to expect a trend change. Add in small clips into stop-loss driven weakness. The same logic applies to the KTB 3Y in Korea.

This week. The only big print is August PCE on 09/30 (core 3.3% y/y prior), which also falls on month and quarter end. A benign number defends the 4.75% floor on the 2Y; a firm one opens another run at 5%.
