02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Direction went missing ahead of the BOK — only the ultra-long end rallied. On 08-25 the KTB 2Y (−0.4bp, 3.693%) and 3Y (−0.6bp, 3.830%) barely moved, while the 20Y (−4.5bp, 4.576%) and 30Y (−4.5bp, 4.632%) richened sharply on their own. The trigger was the primary dealer meeting: smaller 30Y issuance, with buybacks and switches to ease the long-end supply burden. Futures drifted in a narrow range — 3Y +0.07 (103.29), 10Y +0.15 (105.65) — and foreigners turned seller in both legs, −2,470 in 3Y and −2,608 in 10Y.

Oil fell late in the session and US yields followed. WTI settled at 82.36 (−3.12%). The 2Y dropped 5.9bp to 4.175%, the 10Y 6.9bp to 4.629% and the 30Y 6.1bp to 5.166% — around 6bp across the curve — leaving 2-10 at 45.4bp (−1.0bp). The US outperformed Korea, compressing US–KR 10Y to 31.1bp (−5.9bp). PCE and the UST 5Y auction land today, the BOK and Jackson Hole tomorrow.

RV: the 10-30 flattener has reached its first target. At 31.1bp (−3.4bp) it is effectively at the 30.9bp objective and the z-score has normalised to +1.01σ — take-profit territory. 20-30 at 5.4bp now carries a 1.0bp first target. Richness has migrated to 5-10 (28.5bp, +1.79σ) and the 3-5-10 butterfly (7.8bp, +2.28σ) — 5-10 is at its high over the 5, 60 and 250-day windows alike. RV ranks hold at 10-30 (86), 20-30 (83.7) and the 3-10-30 butterfly (78.1); avoids remain the tactical 10Y short (46.2) and the 3-10 steepener (33.3).
