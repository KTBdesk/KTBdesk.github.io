03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Taken on its own, Friday was a front-led bear flattening. The 10/9 close: 2Y 4.790 (+2.8) · 5Y 5.018 (+2.9) · 10Y 5.241 (+1.4) · 20Y 5.6455 (−0.4) · 30Y 5.5975 (−0.4). Thursday's post-auction long-end rally (10/8: 10Y −5.8, 30Y −7.1) was partly given back at the front on Friday. The drivers were Michigan one-year inflation expectations rising from 4.6% to 4.7% (sentiment 46.3 against 47.8 expected) and the September minutes' "one more hike by year-end". The bond market was shut on 10/12 for Columbus Day, and in the meantime WTI reached 93.74 (+2.1%), up 6% in three sessions from 88.28 on 10/8. Gold 4,216 (+1.4%), Dow +0.83%.

The curve stopped at 2-10 45.1bp. After flattening 8bp from 53.2 intraday on 10/8, Friday was quiet at −1.6bp. 2-5 is 22.75bp, a 60-day z of +1.76 — the 2Y and 5Y rose together on Friday, so the change was −0.05 — and the 2-5-10 fly at −0.40 (−1.5) is near its 60-day low of −0.75. The 5Y is still the cheap point, but the risk to this week's trade ① (US 2-5 flattener, currently 22.75) is unchanged — if CPI shakes the December hike, the 2Y rallies first. 10-30 at 35.65 (z −1.29) and 20-30 at −4.80 (z −1.53) keep the back end compressed.

Korea moved 11bp without the US. While the US was shut, Korea's 10Y rallied 11.5bp and KR10−US10 went from −82.9 to −94.4bp (−12.9), a 60-day low at z −2.59. Last week's call — "sell Korea 10Y / buy US 10Y is at target at −82.9, reduce or close" — was right; anyone still in it is 11.5bp offside in one session. Re-entry waits for CPI: if the US cheapens on the print the spread widens further, and if it rallies Korea will not keep up, so tomorrow's number sets the direction.

Nothing new goes on before CPI. Consensus is 3.4% headline (0.4% m/m), 2.4% core (0.3% m/m). With oil up for a third session and Michigan expectations higher, the upside risk is larger than the downside. The 10Y at 5.241 sits at the top of its 60-day range (4.55–5.29) with a one-year z of +2.8, so there is still room for covering, but no outright bet on CPI eve. What stays is the 2-5 flattener (currently 22.75) and the view that the 30Y is a scale-in buy (currently 5.5975) — both noted at current levels only.
