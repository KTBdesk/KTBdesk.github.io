03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Another leg higher after the holiday. On 09-28: 2Y +8.0bp (4.932%) · 5Y +8.4 (5.0715%) · 10Y +7.7 (5.2375%) · 20Y +5.6 (5.6035%) · 30Y +5.8bp (5.5485%). Overnight the 2Y went back above 4.9%, the 10Y above 5.2% and the 30Y above 5.5%. Yields keep setting higher ceilings and dip buying has not shown up — rebounds are met with selling.

Non-policy pressure is the story at the long end. Deficit-driven Treasury supply and heavy big-tech issuance are lifting the term premium. Even if Fed hike expectations soften, the 10Y and 30Y are unlikely to fall as fast as they used to while supply is this heavy. Because long rates are now rising with short rates, there are fewer tenors left that avoid the volatility.

The curve keeps its steepening. 2-10 30.5bp · 2-5 13.9bp, with the September compression to −2σ largely unwound (2-10 z60 −1.13). The long end is the exception — 10-30 31.1bp (one-year z −3.27), 10-20 36.6bp (−3.34) and 20-30 inverted at −5.5bp (z60 −2.51), with the 20Y at 5.6035% still yielding more than the 30Y.

The front is not yet at our level. We treat 4.75% as the floor on the 2Y; at 4.932% it is 18bp away. Cheaper oil (WTI in the low $90s) is easing inflation expectations, but while the Fed tightening watch persists the front will not lead the rally. Our plan is a first small clip above 4.95%, target 4.75%, stop 5.05%.

Tomorrow is the test. August core PCE (3.3% y/y prior) lands on month and quarter end. A benign print sends the 2Y back toward 4.75%; a firm one opens another run at 5%.
