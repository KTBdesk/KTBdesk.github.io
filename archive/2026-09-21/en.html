02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Korea took a hawkish FOMC and a BOJ hike and rallied anyway. Friday: KTB 2Y −0.5bp (3.973) · 3Y −1.8bp (4.027) · 5Y −2.2bp (4.220) · 10Y −3.1bp (4.460) · 20Y −2.1bp (4.485) · 30Y −2.5bp (4.585) — a bull flattening. Futures 3Y +8 ticks (102.29) · 10Y +24 ticks (103.90). On the week 3-10 −7.6bp · 3-30 −11.8bp · 5-30 −6.4bp; month-to-date 10-30 is −13.2bp, the long end flattening most.

Three drivers. ① The BOJ hiked 1.00→1.25% on the 18th, but with two dissents and USD/JPY pushing to 158 despite Ueda leaving back-to-back or 50bp moves on the table, the read was "the pace may be slower than hoped" — the sell-off reversed into a rally. ② Oil fell further. ③ Foreigners bought 13,192 lots of 3Y futures (week +16,322); they were also the buyer on the 16th (+9,538) into FOMC. Month-to-date is still −47,744, so this is the unwind of early-September selling.

The US went the other way. The FOMC hiked 25bp to 4.00% on the 16th with a hawkish dot plot; Friday 2Y +8.2bp (4.747) · 5Y +7.7bp (4.857) · 10Y +6.3bp (4.997) · 30Y +4.0bp (5.327) — a bear flattening. 2Y traded 4.76% intraday, 60-day z +2.66, +40bp on the month; 10Y crossed 5% intraday. October hike odds are ~55%, and Kashkari (Minneapolis) reiterated over the weekend that inflation ex-energy remains broad. Result: KR10−US10 at −53.7bp (3M z −2.24), the widest inversion in the sample — Korea is not following the US front end, and that divergence is the structure of the week.

Every KTB curve is now flatter than −2σ on 60 days. 2-5 24.7bp (−3.69σ) · 2-3 5.4 (−2.87σ) · 3-30 55.8 (−2.60σ) · 3-10 43.3 (−2.41σ) · 3-5 19.3 (−2.35σ) · 10-30 12.5 (−2.12σ). The only steep spot is 20-30 at 10.0bp (+1.28σ) and the 10-20-30 fly at 7.5 (+2.32σ) — with 10-20 compressed to 2.5bp (15.2 on 09-09), the 20Y is the one cheap point. But the 20Y supply has already been and gone — the 20Y 600bn on 09-09 and the 50Y on 09-11 are done, and the 09-10 roll made 26-10 the benchmark. The supply event has passed and the price has not come back, so there is no auction left to wait for.

In the ultra-long end, offshore and locals are going opposite ways. Friday foreigners sold KRW 226.5bn of ultra-longs including −174.5bn of the new 30Y 26-8 (buys only 22-2 +44.1bn · 25-9 +10.2bn), while insurers and pension funds bought +130bn (25-2 +73.8 · 26-2 +42.0 · 26-8 +37.0) plus +40bn of the 24-2 principal strip. 10-30 has come from ~40bp in August to 12.5bp on this real-money demand and the authorities' focus on ultra-long issuance structure; the old 20Y 25-9 now yields less than the 30Y benchmark. Expectations of smaller ultra-long supply in the Nov–Dec plan are partly priced — further flattening from here needs fresh offshore demand beyond WGBI index flows.

Futures are cheap to cash. 3Y implied − cash 3Y +15.3bp (1M +13.7 · 3M z +1.19) · 10Y +5.1bp (+1.64σ); versus fair value 3Y is 3.6 ticks cheap, 10Y 7.5 ticks cheap. Foreigners are buying futures in size and futures still lag cash — cash buying (locals, lending returns) is stronger. Swaps are stronger still: 3Y swap − implied −10.75bp (−2.48σ) · 10Y −29.1bp (−3.40σ) — receivers are ahead.

Policy pricing built up in September and is now backing off. 3Y − base rate 102.7bp (base 3.00%) · 1Y swap − base 71.8bp (MTD +18bp). More hikes were priced over the month; Friday took 2bp out of each. Next BOK MPC 10/22.

Today is the last KTB auction of September. The 5Y new benchmark 26-9, KRW 3.0tn (2.2bp expensive to 26-3, the richest bond in the basket — watch whether it cheapens into supply) plus 91D MSB 700bn. The 20Y 600bn (09-09), the 50Y (09-11) and the 10Y 2.8tn (09-14) have all been absorbed, and there is no KTB supply left this month. Thursday and Friday are Chuseok holidays — three trading days this week, and the US 2/5/7Y auctions fall inside them. September KTB supply is 16.0tn, 1.0tn below August, with only the 30Y up (+200bn). The 20Y is still cheap by +2.32σ on the fly even though its auction has passed, which leaves room for a 20-30 flattener; the front end at 2-5 −3.7σ and US 2-10 at 25bp has little room to flatten further. Money market: call 2.530 · CD91 3.200 · MSB 1Y 3.430%, CD−base at 20bp, +8bp this month.
