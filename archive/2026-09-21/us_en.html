02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Every point on the curve set a fresh one-year high. 2Y +3.8bp (4.434%), 5Y +5.4bp (4.618%), 10Y +5.2bp (4.843%), 20Y +3.9bp (5.292%), 30Y +4.5bp (5.291%) — the 250-day percentile is 100.0% at the 2Y, 5Y and 10Y, and 99.6% at the 20Y and 30Y. Intraday the 10Y traded through 4.85% and the 30Y through 5.29%. Crude did the work, with disappointment over the Treasury buyback layered on top.

Geopolitics pushed crude into triple digits. Iran said it fired ballistic missiles at a US base in Jordan, and both sides reported vessel attacks in Middle East waters, reviving supply-disruption fears. Brent settled 100.71 (+2.85%) — its first triple-digit close since July — and WTI 96.05 (+3.25%), a ninth straight gain. President Trump's remark that the conflict with Iran could run past the midterms extended rather than capped the risk premium.

This geopolitical risk is being read as inflation re-acceleration, not as a flight to safety. So a crude spike transmits through higher breakevens into a wider term premium, and long yields cannot fall easily. The curve shows it: the belly took the most damage, lifting 2-5 to 18.6bp (+1.65), +1.24σ, back above 1σ, while 10-20 at 44.95bp sits at the 0.4th percentile of 250 days, with 10-30 44.85bp (2.4th) and 5-10 22.50bp (2.0th). The cheap point is the belly; the expensive point is the long end.

Views on the Fed are split. A Reuters poll has most economists expecting a hold in September, but after firm payrolls and the crude move, more are allowing for a resumption of hikes after a long pause. What settles it is whether this week's inflation data confirms an underlying slowdown; a strong print takes hike pricing up another notch.

We still lean toward a retracement. PPI consensus is already high at +0.4% MoM and +0.3% core, so an in-line print is hard to trade as fresh weakness; 2-10 is meeting firm resistance around 38bp; and the 10Y auction was well absorbed. On crude, Brent should meet heavy resistance near $103.8 — if you want length, the entry is a 2Y long once it reaches that level. The front rather than the back, because the term premium keeps the long end heavy. If you have been expressing this through the curve, cover the hedge and move to a delta play.
