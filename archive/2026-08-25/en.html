02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

A rally into the MPC — foreigners piled into 3Y futures. On 08-24 KTB cash richened across the belly and long end: 3Y −1.8bp (3.836%), 5Y −4.3bp (4.068%), 10Y −4.1bp (4.335%), 20Y −4.3bp (4.621%), 30Y −2.6bp (4.677%). Futures followed with 3Y +0.09 (103.22) and 10Y +0.39 (105.50). Foreigners drove it with +13,423 contracts in 3Y futures against securities firms at −13,129. The read: back-to-back hikes are seen as already in the price.

The US bull flattened. 10Y −3.6bp (4.698%) with the 2Y unchanged (4.238%) pulled 2-10 in to 46.0bp (−3.6bp). A dense event slate follows — KTB 20Y and UST 2Y auctions today, BOK MPC and Jackson Hole on Thursday, and the US payroll benchmark revision on Friday.

Curve richness has shifted to the belly. 5-30 at 61.1bp (+1.38σ) and 5-10 at 26.6bp (+1.38σ) are now the richest. 3-10 at 49.2bp (−2.3bp) has normalised to +0.97σ and is −2.6bp on the month — no longer the standout it was last week. RV rank 1 stays the 10-30 flattener (86), targeting 34.5→30.6bp; rank 2 the 20-30 flattener (84), at 6.0bp against a 5.8bp target — close enough to consider taking profit; rank 3 the 3-10-30 butterfly (77.4), small size. Avoids: tactical 10Y short (43.8) and 3-10 steepener (31.2).
