The peak of the rally — the BOK meeting where a fall in chip prices is written into the growth forecast. The case for this hike is growth and demand-side pressure, not oil, and the inflation forecasts themselves are not high (core 2.6%). The desk's read is that oil drifts lower with swings, headline inflation slows from next spring and core stays sticky — a path on which long maturities find relief first. The peak is most likely May next year (5/27), February (2/25) at the earliest. → With smaller December supply and new-year money to deploy, buyers on the sidelines have the upper hand until then.
