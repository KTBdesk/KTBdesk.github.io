03 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Only the back end fell on CPI eve. 10/13 close 2Y 4.7925 (+0.3) · 5Y 5.003 (−1.5) · 10Y 5.2105 (−3.1) · 20Y 5.605 (−4.1) · 30Y 5.558 (−4.0). 2-10 went 45.1 → 41.8, 10-30 34.75 (−0.9). On the chart the 10Y is below its 20-day average (5.248) and looking at the lower Bollinger band (5.166) and the bottom of the 60-day box (5.18), while the 2Y sits on its 20-day (4.79) — a bull flattening with the December hike still priced. KOSPI −5.46%, Dow −0.35%, gold −0.6%, and WTI up again at 94.14.

Today's CPI comes after a rally. US 10Y −7.7 MTD (5.2875 → 5.2105), −7.2 on the week. In the six October CPIs since 2020, the two that came after yields had fallen (2023, 2025) saw the 10Y rebound +10 and +40 that week even on a miss, while the two after yields had risen (2020, 2024) saw no reaction to beats. Consensus is headline 0.6% m/m (3.6–3.7% y/y), core 0.2% (2.5%). A beat (core 0.3 or headline 0.7+) means 2Y +7–10 and 10Y back to 5.26–5.29; in line means ±2 then a rebound that week; a miss (headline 0.5 or below, core 0.1) tests 5.16–5.18 — the scenario in which the US follows Korea and Australia lower. With oil up three sessions running, the upside tail is fatter.

KR−US is −99. Korea fell 7.7 while the US fell 3.1, taking KR10−US10 from −94 to −99bp, a 60-day low (z −2.63). That only confirms closing at −82.9 was right; re-entry waits for CPI — if the US breaks 5.18 Korea will not keep up and the spread narrows, if the US rebounds it widens further. The 2-5 flattener (21.05 now, z60 +1.31) and the scale-in 30Y view (5.558) stay at current levels, nothing added before CPI.
