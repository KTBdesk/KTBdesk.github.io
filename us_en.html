02 Macro & Curve Dislocations

Friday was a bear flattening. 2Y +8.2bp (4.747%) · 5Y +7.7bp (4.857%) · 10Y +6.3bp (4.997%) · 20Y +5.1bp (5.375%) · 30Y +4.0bp (5.327%). 2Y is at a one-year high — 60-day z +2.66, +40bp on the month — and 10Y knocked on 5% again, 2Y 4.76% and 10Y above 5% intraday. Most of Thursday's 7–9bp rally was given back in a day.

The Fed is the reason. The FOMC hiked 25bp to 4.00% on the 16th and the dots read hawkish. October hike odds are ~55%, and Kashkari reiterated over the weekend that inflation ex-energy is broad. Add the BOJ hike on the 18th (1.00→1.25%, two dissents, USD/JPY 158) and the global front end is re-pricing the policy path — 2-5 11.1bp · 2-10 25.0bp, a front-end-led flattening.

Every segment is at a 60-day flat. 2-10 25.0bp (−2.45σ · 1M −12.5) · 5-10 13.9 (−2.46σ) · 10-30 33.0 (−2.54σ) · 10-20 37.8 (−2.34σ), and 20-30 inverted at −4.8bp (−2.52σ). 10-30 is −14.6bp month-to-date — the classic flattening of a resumed hiking cycle.

Korea did not follow. KTB 10Y fell 3.1bp to 4.460% the same day, putting KR10−US10 at −53.7bp (3M z −2.24), the widest inversion on record. The dovish BOJ read, lower oil and foreign 3Y futures buying pulled Korea the other way. The gap is unlikely to widen further unless the US front end retraces.

This week — the 2/5/7Y auctions test the 2Y ceiling; PCE comes next Wednesday (09/30). With 2Y mid-range at 4.747 (4.711~4.797), weak 2/5/7Y auctions (ET 22–24, prior 2Y 4.315%) would open 4.80; a benign Aug PCE on the 30th (core 3.3% y/y prior) would unwind hike pricing. For 10Y the range top at 5.03 (09-16 high 5.024) is first resistance. Bands are lifted to 2Y 4.60~4.85 · 10Y 4.85~5.10 — the tops open up only if an October hike is confirmed.
