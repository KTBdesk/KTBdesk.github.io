Then a 1.88% Nasdaq drop (Dow −0.09%) and safe-haven demand turned the long end, 10Y −2.3 and 30Y −3.4 at the close, with hike odds back to 31%. 2-10 38.65 (−3.15), 2-5 18.75 (−2.3) — only the front end, where the hike is priced, rose, and 20-30 at −5.15 (−0.45) shows the 30Y falling most again.

The chart is a test of the lows. The 10Y sits just above the bottom of its 60-day box at 5.18 (5.1875), lower Bollinger band 5.15, below the 20-day average (5.223). The 2Y at 4.801 is above its 20-day (4.796), December still priced. Yesterday's "print after a rally, a beat means a rebound (the 2023 pattern)" held only to +12.5 intraday; the close went the other way — yields that could not rise on a beat, so an in-line-or-softer PPI (0.4 expected) and retail sales (1.2 expected) today breaks 5.18 toward 5.15–5.11 (second support) with the US following Korea and Australia, while a beat retraces to 5.23–5.27.
